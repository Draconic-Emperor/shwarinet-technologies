# ShwariNet Technologies

ShwariNet Technologies is a modern IT services platform focused on helping homes, students, and small businesses improve their technology experience through Wi-Fi testing, network setup, cybersecurity awareness, and IT support.

## Services

- 📶 Wi-Fi Testing & Optimization
- 🌐 Network Setup & Troubleshooting
- 🔒 Basic Cybersecurity Assessments
- 💻 IT Support & Technical Assistance
- 🖨️ Printer & Device Configuration
- ⚙️ Windows Installation & Maintenance

To provide affordable, reliable, and professional technology services that help people get the most out of their internet, devices, and digital infrastructure.
